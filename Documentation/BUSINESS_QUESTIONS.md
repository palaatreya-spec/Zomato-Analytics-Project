# Zomato Restaurant Analytics — Business Questions

This project is structured around questions a restaurant-platform analytics team could use to evaluate restaurant supply, customer-facing quality signals, pricing proxies, and order performance.

## Revenue & Performance

1. Which restaurants generate the highest order revenue?
2. Which locations contribute the most revenue and order volume?
3. What share of revenue is concentrated in the top restaurants?
4. Which cuisines combine strong restaurant presence with strong revenue?

## Pricing & Customer Value

5. How does listed cost-for-two vary across cuisines and locations?
6. Do higher-priced restaurants also show higher order value?
7. Which restaurant segments have high order volume but relatively low order value?

## Quality & Engagement

8. How do restaurant ratings relate to order activity?
9. Which restaurants have strong order activity despite below-average ratings?
10. Which cuisines have the strongest combination of rating and restaurant availability?

## Operating Model

11. How does online-order availability differ across locations and cuisines?
12. How does table-booking availability differ across locations and cuisines?
13. Are restaurants offering digital ordering or table booking concentrated in particular markets?

## Portfolio Prioritization

14. Which restaurants fall into high-volume/high-value, high-volume/low-value, low-volume/high-value, and low-volume/low-value segments?
15. Which locations have a large restaurant base but relatively weak average performance?

## Decision framework

The analysis should move from:

**Metric → Pattern → Business interpretation → Recommendation**

Observational relationships are not treated as causal without supporting evidence.
