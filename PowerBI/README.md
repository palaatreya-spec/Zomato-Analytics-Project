# Power BI — Zomato Restaurant Market Analytics

## Data source

Use the cleaned source-backed table/dataset generated by the Python pipeline. The dashboard must not connect to the legacy customer/order/review model.

## Report pages

### 01 — Executive Market Overview

**KPI cards**
- Restaurant Count
- Rated Restaurants
- Average Rating
- Median Cost for Two
- Online Order Adoption %
- Table Reservation Adoption %

**Primary visuals**
- Top 15 cities by restaurant count
- City restaurant count vs average rating scatter
- Rating distribution
- Online ordering vs reservation adoption

### 02 — City Intelligence

**Slicers:** City, Area, Price Band, Rating Band, Online Order, Table Reservation

**Visuals**
- City KPI matrix
- Restaurant supply ranking
- Average rating by city
- Median cost-for-two by city
- Digital adoption by city
- Rating-count engagement by city

### 03 — Restaurant Performance

**Visuals**
- Performance Score distribution
- Top 20 restaurants by score
- Rating vs rating-count scatter
- Performance segment breakdown
- Restaurant detail table

**Drill-through fields**
- Restaurant name
- City
- Area
- Cuisine
- Rating
- Rating count
- Cost for two
- Online ordering
- Table reservation
- Performance score

### 04 — Cuisine Intelligence

**Slicers:** City, Price Band, Rating Band

**Visuals**
- Top cuisines by restaurant count
- Cuisine rating comparison
- Cuisine pricing comparison
- Cuisine digital adoption
- Cuisine engagement

## Design principles

- Keep the report clean and recruiter-friendly.
- Prefer horizontal bars and scatter plots over pie charts for high-cardinality categories.
- Use consistent units and number formats.
- Avoid overcrowding KPI pages.
- Use drill-through instead of placing every restaurant-level field on the overview.
- Add a small methodology note: listed cost-for-two is a pricing proxy, not realized revenue.
- Add a data-quality note for unrated records and invalid coordinates.

Before publishing the report:

- [ ] Restaurant Count reconciles to source

- [ ] Average Rating reconciles to SQL/Python
